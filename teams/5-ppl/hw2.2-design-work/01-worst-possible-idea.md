# Exercise 1: Worst possible idea

## Original team idea: A floating hat

**Problem framing added for this write-up:** A student wants to wear something fun outdoors or at a party without carrying another object.

**Bad idea:** A hat that floats over your head.

**Additions from our notes:**

- The hat has holes in it.
- It floats three feet above your head.
- The battery lasts only five minutes.

**Redemptions from our notes:**

- The low battery life means you do not have to use it for long because it sucks.
- It could be a cool party trick.
- Putting a camera on top could be cool.
- You get to feel the rain and enjoy it.

## Flipping each part

| Bad feature | What could be useful | Flipped idea |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A hat that floats | It gets attention and starts conversations. | A simple party activity that helps people interact, without needing flying hardware. |
| Holes in the hat | Airflow could make it less hot to wear. Feeling the rain could also be part of the experience. | A breathable cap, or a tool for planning outdoor activities based on how much rain someone enjoys. |
| Three feet above your head | A higher camera angle might capture a group. | Start with a phone stand or cap-mounted camera instead of a floating device. |
| Five-minute battery | A short activity can be enough for a joke or quick clip. | A short party game or a camera that records only the moment someone wants. |
| It is too useless to wear for long | The original joke points toward a novelty use. | Treat it as a brief party experience rather than everyday clothing. |

The main useful directions are helping people have fun together, capturing shared moments, and making outdoor plans around the weather. Actual floating hardware would be difficult to build and test during this course.

## Four additional bad ideas and flips

These are AI-assisted additions for this submission. They are not attributed to individual teammates or presented as recovered class notes.

| Underlying problem | Bad idea | Make it worse | Flip |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| People leave an event without getting everyone’s photos. | A party camera that only photographs the tops of hats. | It deletes photos after five minutes and needs a new app for every guest. | A shared event album where guests upload selected photos through one link. |
| A host struggles to get guests talking. | A party hat that loudly announces everyone’s embarrassing stories. | It interrupts every conversation and repeats the same joke. | An optional set of short activities that guests can skip or join. |
| Students need to decide when to walk outside. | A weather app that tells you it rained only after you get home. | It always says to bring nothing and offers no timing choices. | A planner that compares forecast rain across the times a student could leave. |
| Someone wants to record a quick hands-free clip. | A camera hat that turns off as soon as recording starts. | The camera points backward and the battery cannot be replaced. | A simple adjustable phone or camera mount, with a clear recording indicator and battery reminder. |
