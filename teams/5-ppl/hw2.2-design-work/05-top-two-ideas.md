# Our proposed top two ideas

These are AI-assisted recommendations based on the generated concepts, simulated votes, and Lean Canvases. The team’s actual voting and final choice still need confirmation. The problems below are hypotheses for interviews, not findings from completed research.

## 1. PartyLens

After a small event, the photos may be spread across everyone’s phones, and the host has to ask people to send them. This problem could affect adult students hosting birthdays, club socials, or other gatherings. They would care because collecting the pictures takes effort, and some good moments may never reach the rest of the group. PartyLens would give guests one link to upload selected photos into a shared event album.

This idea grows out of the camera and party-trick flips from the floating hat. The first version would focus on creating an album, uploading photos, and choosing who can view it. We would ask 10 target users about their latest events and whether their current photo-sharing tools already solve the problem.

## 2. RainWindow

A weather forecast may show rain without making it easy to choose the best time to leave. This problem could affect adult students who walk to campus and can leave at more than one time. They would care because arriving with wet clothes or a wet bag is frustrating, but comparing several forecast time slots takes effort. RainWindow would compare estimated rain during the times they could leave and show the tradeoffs clearly.

This idea grows out of the hat’s holes and the thought of feeling and enjoying the rain. It gives users a choice about their outdoor plans instead of assuming everyone wants the same experience. We would ask 10 target users how rain affected a recent trip, how flexible their schedules were, and whether a simple comparison would add anything to their current weather app.
